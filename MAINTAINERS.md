# Maintainers

`diff_viz` is maintained by the Tinkora organization. The current administrative
maintainer is [@tinkeragora](https://github.com/tinkeragora).

Maintainers are expected to:

- Keep project scope, lifecycle state, and public claims accurate.
- Triage contributions and resolve review conversations.
- Run and record required checks before merge or release.
- Apply least privilege and keep sensitive material out of public repositories.
- Coordinate deprecation, incidents, and translations.

Project maintainer access requires sustained, trustworthy contributions, a clear
ongoing responsibility, and organization-owner approval. Access is reviewed when
responsibilities change, a maintainer becomes inactive, or a security concern
arises.
